# Cross-tier fidelity review: round 1 (2026-10-29)

**Setup.**
- Three independent reviewers, on Opus, Sonnet and Haiku.
- None had seen the drafting conversation, and none saw each other's output.
- Each audited spec v0.3 + D-047 to D-054 against the [vision ledger](../10-vision-ledger.md).

## Verdicts

| Tier | Bottom line | Covered | Partial | Deferred | Contradicted | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opus | **Drifting, not off the rails** | 13 | 9 | 2 | 2 (VR-06, VR-10) | 1 (VR-22) |
| Sonnet | **Drifting** | 13 | 10 (+ VR-24 "at risk") | 2 | 1 (VR-06, in effect) | 0 (VR-22 rated partial) |
| Haiku | **Drifting, recoverable before spec 1.0** | 14 | 10 | 1 | 1 (VR-10) | 1 (VR-22) |

## Where all three agree

**Faithful:** the lens (VR-01), vocabulary hidden (04), hypothesis-and-test structure (05), intake
→ spec (11), scope control (16), repo tracking (17), the sheaf code and Go port (18), open source
(19), plug and play (21), naming (25).

**Drifting:**
1. The rendered artifact was neither measured nor gated (VR-02/03).
2. Speed went backwards and was unmeasured (VR-06).
3. Self-adaptation was removed from product runs for eval-hygiene reasons that only apply to eval
   (VR-08/10/12).
4. VR-00 narrowed to a research skill with no inventory of v1 elements.
5. The fidelity review itself (VR-22) was cited but never defined.

## Resolution

- **Applied as D-055 to D-061.**
- **Left for the owner:** cross-run learning in product runs, the memory bank in v2.0 vs v2.1, the
  rigor vs speed default, and the v1 port phasing.
